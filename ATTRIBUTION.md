## What You Cannot Do

- Remove John Tan's name from the boards or any derived work
- Claim you created the original resource
- Apply a more restrictive licence to adapted versions
- Sell the original boards without adaptation (CC BY 4.0 permits commercial use of adaptations, but the original resource is free)

## Questions About Attribution

If you are uncertain whether your use requires attribution or how to  
credit the resource in a specific context, the answer is: yes, it  
requires attribution, and the standard text above covers every case.

For collaboration, translation partnerships, or adaptation support,  
contact the author via ORCID or ResearchGate.
